Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install any app on the TV?
No. PUBSHOW runs right in the TV browser. Open the TV Player link on any Smart TV, Chromecast or laptop connected to the screen. No app, no installation.
Does the customer need to download anything to use the Jukebox?
No. The customer scans the QR code with their phone camera and it opens right in the browser — no app, no sign-up, no password.
How do I get the money from Jukebox requests?
The customer pays straight to your account via Stripe. The money is yours — PUBSHOW takes no cut of the requests.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contract, no penalty, no hassle. Cancel from the dashboard or message support.
